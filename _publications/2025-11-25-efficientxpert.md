EfficientXpert converts a general pretrained model into a sparse, domain-adapted expert through a single pruning step. It combines ForeSight Mask, a propagation-aware pruning criterion, with Partial Brain Surgeon, an efficient update for low-rank adapters under a fixed sparsity pattern.
