I am Songlin Zhao, currently affiliated with the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Before joining UIUC, I studied mathematics and statistics at the University of California, Berkeley.

My research interests include efficient machine learning and randomized linear algebra. My work spans efficient large language models, photonic computing, and the evaluation of AI-generated content.

You can find my work on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=wcOlMykAAAAJ&hl=en), [GitHub](https://github.com/TagoreZhao), and [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-2824-9115).
